# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS. No build step, no framework, deployable to any static host (Netlify, Vercel, Cloudflare Pages, GitHub Pages). Lives in `night-window/`, separate from the Burger James site at the repo root.

## Users

Late-night eaters, mostly 18 to 35: people leaving a shift, a show, a bar, or a study session between roughly 9 PM and 3 AM. They are on a phone, often outside, often hungry right now, deciding in seconds whether the window is open and what to get.

## Product Purpose

A one-page site for Night Window, a walk-up smash burger counter that only opens at night. The site answers three questions fast: is it open right now, what's on the short menu, and where is the window. Success is a visitor who walks up (or calls ahead) knowing exactly what they want.

## Positioning

The window is the restaurant. No dining room, no table service: a lit service window on a dark street, a flat-top, and a menu short enough to memorize. It opens when everything else closes.

## Operating Context

- Visited almost entirely on phones, at night, frequently outdoors or in a car.
- Typical session: check hours, glance at the menu, get directions, leave.
- Ordering is at the window or by phone; there is no online ordering system.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Concept / portfolio piece. The business is fictional.
- Short menu (about five items plus sides and drinks), walk-up and call-ahead only.
- Undecided / not real: address, phone number, prices, hours. Any shown on the site are placeholders and must be labeled as sample content, never presented as real.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Night Window (fictional).
- Voice: short, dry, warm, a little nocturnal. Talks like the person at the window, not like an ad.

## Evidence on Hand

- No real reviews, press, ratings, or customer quotes exist. Do not fabricate any, and do not imply real social proof.
- Food and storefront photography will be generated for this concept and must be described as concept imagery in the handoff.

## Product Principles

1. Open-or-closed is the first thing anyone should learn.
2. The menu is short on purpose; the site should be too.
3. Honest about being a concept: sample details are labeled, nothing fake is presented as proof.
4. Built for a phone held one-handed at 1 AM.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Night viewing means a dark-friendly palette, but body text must still hit 4.5:1 contrast. Respect reduced motion.
