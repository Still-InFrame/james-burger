# Surface brief: Night Window home

Scope: the one-page site, `night-window/index.html`. Visitor mode: Persuade.

Audience: late-night eaters on a phone, outdoors, deciding in seconds. Action: walk up or call ahead, in working form (live open/closed status, call button, directions). Proof: the window itself, the short menu, the hours. No reviews or ratings exist; none are shown. All business details are labeled sample content.

Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, self-hosted fonts, reduced motion respected, WCAG AA contrast on every sodium and umber ground.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is lit the way the street is lit at 2 AM: one sodium lamp, one glowing window, everything else in warm shadow. It refuses the category default of a black page with a neon accent and a burger floating on it.

OWN-WORLD: Drenched sodium amber (#F29A2E) fields and burnt-umber shadow (#1A0C05), never neutral black or gray. Gold-leaf sign-painter lettering on dark glass (Ultra display, Yellowtail script for the hours line), Barlow for working text. Photos are monochrome sodium-lit night.

STORY: Visitor learns instantly whether the window is open, sees a menu short enough to memorize, believes it is a real place on a real corner, and calls ahead or walks up.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed photo of the lit service window across a dark street. Over the glass area, HTML lettering: NIGHT WINDOW wordmark, script line "smash burgers till 3", a live status plate (OPEN NOW / CLOSED, opens 9 PM) and two actions: Call ahead (primary, sodium plate) and Directions. Signature interaction: the lamp. When closed, the hero shows the shuttered window; "Flip the light" warms the scene up like a real sodium lamp (dim red, then amber) and swaps to the lit window.

FORM: Sodium Streetlight, my grounded rank 1 (user chose it over the assigned rank 4). seed:537e7f7b79bf2f39
